A NEW LEADERSHIP JOURNEY

Experience leadership from the inside

As part of our Talent Development Strategy, we are proud to launch HORIZONS, a unique leadership development experience designed to accelerate the growth of future operational leaders through real-world business challenges and international exposure. Over an 18-month international journey, participants will work alongside senior leaders in some of the Group's most strategic operations, gaining hands-on leadership experience while contributing to real business transformation. More than a program, it's a unique opportunity to experience leadership from the inside, broaden your perspective and grow through real operational challenges. Participants will progressively take on broader responsibilities, strengthen their leadership capabilities and develop the business acumen required to prepare for future General Manager and CEO positions.

We believe leadership is built through ambition, trust and experience. HORIZONS is designed to help participants broaden their perspective, strengthen their leadership capabilities and accelerate their development through immersive operational experiences.
